## 📌 Prerequisite Checklist Before Starting

Before Google Play Console will allow you to activate In-App Products:
1. You **MUST** upload your first `.aab` production build to at least one track (e.g. **Internal Testing** track).
2. Your Google Play Console account must have a **Google Merchant Account** linked (*Setup → Payment profile*).

---

## 🚀 STEP 1: Upload Your Build to Internal Testing Track

Google Play requires your app package to declare the billing permission (`com.android.vending.BILLING`) before you can create products.

1. Go to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Select your application: **Quota Hire** (`com.oziegbedavid.quotahire`).
3. In the left sidebar, under **Testing**, click **Internal testing**.
4. Click **Create new release** (top right).
5. Drag and drop your `.aab` file generated by running:
   ```bash
   eas build --platform android --profile production
   ```
6. Enter release notes (e.g., `Initial IAP test build`).
7. Click **Save**, then click **Next**, and finally click **Start rollout to Internal testing**.

---

## 💳 STEP 2: Create the In-App Product (`cv_download_150`)

1. In the left navigation menu, scroll down to the **Monetize** section.
2. Click on **In-app products** (under *Products*).
3. Click the blue **Create product** button.

### 📝 Fill in the exact details below:

#### A. Product Details
- **Product ID**: `cv_download_150`  
  *(⚠️ CRITICAL: Must be exact lower-case string — matches line 42 of `native-payment-modal.tsx` and Django backend settings).*
- **Name**: `CV Download (PDF Export)`
- **Description**: `One-time fee to export and download your professional CV in high-resolution PDF format.`

#### B. Pricing
1. Click **Set price**.
2. Set your **Default price**:
   - Enter `1.50` (or `1500` NGN / your base currency equivalent).
   - Base Currency: Select **EUR (€)** or **USD ($)** or **NGN (₦)**.
3. Click **Update tax and exchange rates** so Google automatically calculates local prices for all 170+ supported countries.
4. Click **Apply price**.

#### C. Save & Activate
1. Click **Save** at the bottom right.
2. The product is now saved as *Draft*. Click the **Activate** button in the top banner or bottom bar.
3. Verify that the product status shows **Active** with a green badge.

## 🔑 STEP 3: Create Google Cloud Service Account (For Server Verification)

Your Render Django backend needs permission to verify purchases with Google Play API server-side.

1. Go to the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/).
2. Select the Google Cloud project linked to your Google Play Console.
3. In the top search bar, search for **Google Play Android Developer API** and click **Enable**.
4. Navigation Menu → **IAM & Admin** → **Service Accounts**.
5. Click **+ CREATE SERVICE ACCOUNT** at the top.
6. Fill in:
   - **Service account name**: `quotahire-iap-verifier`
   - **Service account ID**: `quotahire-iap-verifier` *(auto-filled)*
   - **Description**: `Verifies Google Play billing tokens for Quota Hire backend`
7. Click **CREATE AND CONTINUE**.
8. Under **Select a role**, choose: `Google Play Android Developer` (or `Editor`).
9. Click **DONE**.
10. Find your newly created service account in the list, click the **three dots (⋮)** on the right → **Manage keys**.
11. Click **ADD KEY** → **Create new key**.
12. Select **JSON** format and click **CREATE**.
13. A `.json` file will download to your computer. Keep this file safe!

---

## 🛡️ STEP 4: Grant Play Console Permissions to the Service Account

1. Return to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. In the left navigation menu, click **Setup** → **API access**.
3. Under *Service accounts*, locate `quotahire-iap-verifier@...`.
4. Click **Grant permissions** (or *Manage permissions*).
5. In the **App permissions** tab, click **Add app** and select **Quota Hire**.
6. In the **Account permissions** tab, check the following permissions:
   - ✅ **View financial data, orders, and cancellation responses**
   - ✅ **Manage orders and subscriptions**
7. Click **Invite user** / **Save permissions** at the bottom right.

## 🧪 STEP 6: Configure License Testing (Free Test Purchases)

To test purchases on your phone without spending real money:

1. In Google Play Console, go to **Setup** → **License testing**.
2. Under **License testers**, add your personal Gmail address (`oziegbedavid048@gmail.com` or your test email).
3. Under **License response**, select **RESPOND_NORMALLY**.
4. Click **Save changes**.
